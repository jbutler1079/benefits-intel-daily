### Kaufman Hall: hospital bad debt and charity care ran 17% higher year-to-date through June 2026 than the same period in 2025, even as operating margins held roughly flat

- **Source:** Kaufman Hall (now published under Vizient) National Hospital Flash Report, June 2026 data, covering 1,300+ hospitals via Strata Decision Technology. Report itself dated to late June/July 2026 release cycle; cited directly in coverage published Aug 26–27, 2026 (Advisory Board Daily Briefing, Aug 27; FierceHealthcare). Primary kaufmanhall.com/vizient.com report page redirected during direct fetch, so this is sourced via two independent secondary outlets reporting the same underlying figures — moderate-high confidence, not yet independently confirmed against the raw PDF.
- **Methodology/sample:** Monthly hospital financial-performance index built from claims/financial data across 1,300+ U.S. hospitals, compiled by Strata Decision Technology for Kaufman Hall/Vizient; June 2026 data point, year-to-date comparison against H1 2025.
- **The number:** June 2026 operating margin index was **4.5%** (up 6% from May), but the **calendar-year-to-date margin index of 2.5% was down 6% versus the first half of 2025**. Meanwhile, **daily bad debt and charity care ran 17% higher year-to-date** than the same period last year, and year-to-date total expenses were 6% higher than the prior year — with smaller and rural hospitals flagged as under the most cash-reserve strain.
- **Why it matters:** Rising uncompensated care and expense growth are the standard justification hospitals give for pushing higher commercial rates onto payers and self-funded plans — this is a real-time, primary-dataset confirmation that the underlying cost pressure (not just negotiating posture) is intensifying, which strengthens the case for self-funded sponsors to scrutinize whether commercial rate increases are proportionate to actual hospital cost growth rather than accepted at face value, and adds urgency to reference-based-pricing/direct-contracting conversations as a hedge against cost-shift.
- **Social angle:** Chart pairing margin stability (4.5%, flat-ish) against the 17% bad-debt/charity spike — "Hospitals say costs are rising. The data agrees — but ask where that gets passed." Good for a chart-led LinkedIn post aimed at CFOs renewing plans for 2027.
- **Direct-contracting/RBP tie-in flag:** Indirect — supports the broader cost-shift argument underlying the RBP/direct-contracting pitch, not TX-APCD-specific.
