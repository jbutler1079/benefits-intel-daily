### Specialty drug utilization is the trend driver: net specialty cost +12.5% in 2025, members on a specialty drug up from 4.4% to 5.5% (2023→2026 report)
- **Source:** Pharmaceutical Strategies Group, 2026 State of Specialty Spend and Trend Report (as summarized by HCA Mag, Oct 2026 — primary report not read)
- **Methodology/sample:** Not stated in the secondary coverage; PSG report covers net specialty spend and utilization trend.
- **The number:** Net specialty drug cost +12.5% in 2025; share of members using at least one specialty drug rose 4.4% (2023) → 5.5%. Growth driven by more users and new indications, not unit price.
- **Why it matters:** A negotiated discount per unit is swamped by volume: ~25% of employer spend is pharmacy (BGH 2027 survey, n=127), and utilization growth of ~25% in specialty users over two years erodes any rebate/discount win. Supports a stop-loss and specialty-sourcing conversation for self-funded groups, and pass-through/transparent PBM contracting. *Unbundled/direct-contracting tie: moderate (pharmacy carve-out angle).*
- **Social angle:** Simple two-bar chart (4.4% vs 5.5% of members) with caption "Price per drug isn't the problem; the number of people on specialty drugs is." Verify the PSG primary report before posting.

### Nearly two-thirds of self-funded employers have specialty rebate agreements with a *guarantee* — and researchers warn guarantees may obscure net price and steer formularies to higher-cost drugs
- **Source:** American Journal of Managed Care research (date and sample not stated in coverage; reported by HCA Mag, Oct 2026 — primary article not located)
- **Methodology/sample:** Unknown from secondary source; reported as employers with self-funded pharmacy benefits.
- **The number:** ~2/3 of surveyed self-funded employers report specialty rebate guarantees.
- **Why it matters:** A headline guaranteed rebate can coexist with a higher net cost if it nudges formulary toward high list-price products — exactly the conflict transparent/pass-through PBM models and audit rights are meant to address. *Ties to PBM purchasing standards (PBGH/Transparent RX positioning).*
- **Social angle:** LinkedIn take: "A rebate guarantee is a revenue number, not a net-cost number. Ask for net cost per script." Low confidence on stats until primary AJMC piece is confirmed.
