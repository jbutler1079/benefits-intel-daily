### Employers with full claims-data access are nearly 2x more confident in hospital billing integrity than those without it
- **Source:** National Alliance of Healthcare Purchaser Coalitions, "2026 Pulse of the Purchaser" survey, press release dated August 12, 2026. Distributed via [National Alliance](https://www.nationalalliancehealth.org/news/employers-with-claims-data-access-take-more-action-on-healthcare-costs-national-alliance-survey-finds/) and [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/employers-with-claims-data-access-take-more-action-on-healthcare-costs-national-alliance-of-healthcare-purchaser-coalitions-survey-finds-302849684.html); independently corroborated by [FierceHealthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/claims-data-transparency-empowers-employers-act-affordability-survey), [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/employers-with-claims-data-access-take-more-cost-action-survey-finds), and [Healthcare Finance News](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/employers-increasingly-eyeing-more-transparent-pbms). Direct WebFetch to the primary press release was blocked by this session's egress proxy; the numbers below are cross-confirmed across all four independent outlets, which is treated as sufficient verification in place of a direct fetch.
- **Methodology/sample:** Online survey of 408 employers, fielded May–June 2026, spanning manufacturing, education, public administration, finance/insurance, and healthcare/social assistance sectors (24.4% with 10,000+ employees, 37.5% with 1,000–9,999, 38.1% with fewer than 1,000).
- **The number:** Employers with full claims-data access report **71% confidence in hospital billing integrity**, vs. just **37%** among those without full access — a 34-point gap. Separately, "transparent PBM" adoption more than doubled year-over-year, from **12% to 31%** (Big Three PBM usage fell from 72% to 61%), and employers on transparent PBMs were more likely to report lower premiums (42% vs. 27%).
- **Why it matters:** This is a primary-source, employer-reported validation of the core unbundled/direct-contracting pitch: plan sponsors who actually own and can see their claims data act more decisively on hospital and PBM cost drivers, and trust what they're seeing at roughly twice the rate of those locked out of their own data. Directly supports HPHP's data-ownership/transparency positioning — flagging prominently per the unbundled/direct-contracting criterion.
- **Social angle:** Stat-contrast card — "71% vs. 37%: confidence in hospital billing integrity, with vs. without full claims-data access." Caption: "You can't manage what you can't see." Strong LinkedIn post for a benefits-broker/employer audience; pairs with the 12%→31% transparent-PBM adoption trend as a follow-up post.
