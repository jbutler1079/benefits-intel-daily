### Employers still aren't executing on biosimilar/GLP-1 cost levers even as PBM trust holds — CVS Caremark survey
- **Source:** CVS Caremark & Employee Benefit News, "State of Pharmacy Management Survey," released August 14, 2026. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-study-from-cvs-caremark-and-employee-benefit-news-shows-how-employers-rely-on-their-pharmacy-benefit-manager-to-create-sustainable-pharmacy-solutions-302849265.html) | [CVS Health newsroom](https://www.cvshealth.com/news/pbm/new-study-from-cvs-caremark-and-employee-benefit-news.html) | [Chain Drug Review coverage](https://chaindrugreview.com/employers-lean-on-pbms-as-pharmacy-costs-climb/) | [StockTitan summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CVS/new-study-from-cvs-caremark-and-employee-benefit-news-shows-how-ycp76giijmcx.html)
- **Methodology/sample:** Employer-side HR/benefits decision-maker survey fielded by CVS Caremark in partnership with Employee Benefit News. Exact sample size and field dates could not be recovered — nearly every syndication domain was blocked to WebFetch today, so this is built from WebSearch snippets only, not the primary document. **Note:** this is a vendor-sponsored survey (CVS Caremark is a PBM promoting PBM value) — treat as directional, not independent research.
- **The number:** 91% of employers are concerned about high medication prices for employees; 88% believe PBMs are well-positioned to reduce drug costs. On GLP-1s: 77% call high GLP-1 coverage cost a concern, and 80% have either already limited or are considering limiting GLP-1 coverage for weight loss. On biosimilars: only 49% of employers currently encourage biosimilar substitution and just 12% educate employees on them, despite documented savings potential.
- **Why it matters:** The 49%/12% biosimilar-adoption gap is the sharpest data point here — a quantified, self-reported admission that half of employers are leaving PBM-negotiated biosimilar savings on the table. That's a direct opening for an unbundled/direct-contracting pitch built around formulary and biosimilar-steerage transparency that the incumbent PBM isn't proactively surfacing. The 80% GLP-1-limiting figure also confirms GLP-1 cost pressure is now a majority-action item, not an outlier concern.
- **Social angle:** "Half of employers aren't pushing biosimilars — even though 91% are worried about drug costs" stat-card post; pair the 49%/12% figures with a one-line explainer on what a fiduciary/direct-contracting model does differently. Good candidate for a simple bar-chart graphic (91% concerned → 49% acting on biosimilars → 12% educating employees) showing the "concern-to-action" drop-off.
