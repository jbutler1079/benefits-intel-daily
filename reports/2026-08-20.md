### Regeneron's Pasatru (garetosmab) FDA approval is a fresh, concrete illustration of what a single specialty-drug claim now costs a self-funded plan
- **Source:** FDA approval, August 19, 2026, for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP). Independently corroborated same-day by [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/regeneron-fop-garetosmab-fda-approval-pasatru/), [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3682176), FiercePharma, Endpoints News, Investing.com, and MarketScreener — six independent outlets.
- **Methodology/sample:** Approval based on Phase 3 OPTIMA trial data — a 90–94% reduction in new heterotopic ossification lesions and a significant reduction in flare-ups through 56 weeks.
- **The number:** Annual list price of **$693,000 to $2.1 million**, depending on weight-based dosing (10 mg/kg IV monthly standard dose, or a reduced 3 mg/kg dose for tolerability).
- **Why it matters:** FOP is ultra-rare (roughly 1 in 2 million people), so direct plan exposure is very low probability — but that's exactly the point for stop-loss conversations: a single covered member on this drug is a $700K–$2M+ annual claim landing squarely in laser/specific-deductible territory, arriving with essentially no warning. It's a concrete, dated data point for "this is what one claim looks like now" content aimed at self-funded plan sponsors evaluating specific-deductible levels and stop-loss carrier selection.
- **Social angle:** Stat-card format — "$2.1M/year: one new drug approval, one potential claim." Pairs well with existing stop-loss trend commentary (Sun Life's reported 29%/47% jump in $1M+/$3M+ claims, Voya's ~24% renewal increases) as a follow-up post connecting rising catastrophic-claim frequency to the pipeline of six/seven-figure specialty drugs entering the market.
- **Caveat — flagging honestly rather than over-selling:** This does **not** tie to unbundled/direct-contracting positioning or the TX-APCD data gap specifically; it's a specialty-pharmacy/stop-loss illustration, not a hospital-pricing or PBM-contracting finding. Including it because it clears the bar on specificity, dating, and corroboration, not because it's central to HPHP's core direct-contracting pitch.
