### Alternative PBMs' satisfaction edge over the Big 3 has nearly vanished: gap shrank from ~1.1 points to ~0.2
- **Source:** Pharmaceutical Strategies Group (PSG), 2026 Pharmacy Benefit Manager Customer Satisfaction Report, as summarized by Drug Channels (Oct 20, 2026). A PSG figure from this report appeared in the 2026-09-20 report as sentiment color; the non-Big-3 trend below was not reported.
- **Methodology/sample:** Survey of 250 benefits leaders (employers, health plans, health systems, Taft-Hartley plans) covering about 144 million lives; nearly two-thirds used a Big 3 PBM. Satisfaction on a 10-point scale. Vendor-consultant survey, not claims data. I read the Drug Channels summary, not the PSG report itself.
- **The number:** Non-Big-3 PBM customer satisfaction fell from 7.9 to 7.3, while Big 3 customers sit at 7.1, so the gap is about 0.2 points. Non-Big-3 Net Promoter Score fell from +16 to +4 (Big 3 stayed at -22). Likelihood of renewing three years without an RFP: 6.8 for non-Big-3 vs 6.2 for Big 3. Over 80% of respondents are at least moderately interested in including a non-Big-3 PBM in their next procurement, and 69% did so in their last RFP.
- **Why it matters:** Employers are moving to "alternative" PBMs, but switching vendors is not the same as getting a transparent contract. Drug Channels notes that changing the name on the contract does not necessarily remove every economic tie to a Big 3 PBM. The same pattern applies to medical TPAs: the contract terms and data access matter more than the logo. Non-Big-3 PBMs still rated higher on all six transparency measures, with revenue sources showing the largest gap, but revenue transparency was the lowest-rated area for every PBM.
- **Social angle:** LinkedIn take: "Leaving the Big 3 PBMs? Satisfaction with the alternatives dropped from 7.9 to 7.3. Moving the logo isn't the fix. Pass-through terms, audit rights and data ownership are." Label it as survey sentiment, and do not present it as a price finding.
- **Flags:** Loosely ties to unbundled/direct-contracting positioning (contract structure over vendor brand). TX-APCD angle: none.
